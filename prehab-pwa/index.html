      Resources

      • ERAS Society Guidelines — see latest specialty-specific recommendations on the ERAS Society website.
      • ESPEN (nutrition) — perioperative nutrition screening, energy 25–30 kcal/kg/day, protein generally 1.2–1.5 g/kg/day based on clinical context.
      • Smoking and alcohol cessation ideally ≥4 weeks pre-op; support with behavioral and pharmacologic aids.
      • Pre-op fasting: clear fluids up to 2 hours; solids up to 6 hours unless otherwise instructed.

      For exact protocols, follow your center’s ERAS pathway and consult the primary literature. This app summarizes common principles without quoting proprietary content.
